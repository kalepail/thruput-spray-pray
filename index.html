What are we measuring?

Successful ERC20 transfers committed on the connected Thruput network. Live TPS is the last five seconds of successful receipt observations divided by five. Average TPS includes signing, submission, and receipt drain, excluding idle gaps.

Each emitted particle represents one attempted transfer of 0.0000001 HOG. Particles show submission, not confirmation. Old particles are recycled when the canvas reaches its capacity.

RPC acceptance is not success. Unresolved means no terminal receipt was observed within 60 seconds. Test wallet keys are deterministic and public. Session statistics live in server memory.

Flow control pauses new submissions while the outstanding-work budget is full. It includes work being signed and transfers awaiting receipts. This is normal backpressure, not a transaction error. Receipt delay measures submission to observed execution, including RPC and polling time.

The server sets the starting values. More requests can increase RPC and receipt contention rather than throughput.
